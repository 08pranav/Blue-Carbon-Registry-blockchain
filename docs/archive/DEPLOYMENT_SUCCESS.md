## 🧪 **Test Your System NOW!**

### **1. Visit the Blockchain Page**
Open your browser and go to: **[http://localhost:8080/blockchain](http://localhost:8080/blockchain)**

### **2. Connect MetaMask**
1. Make sure MetaMask is on **Sepolia testnet**
2. Import your account using the private key from .env if needed
3. Click "Connect MetaMask" on the blockchain page

### **3. Test NGO Registration**
1. Go to the **Register tab**
2. Fill in:
   - **NGO Name**: "Test NGO Demo"
   - **Documents Hash**: "QmTestHash123456789"
3. Click **"Register NGO"**
4. **Confirm the MetaMask transaction**
5. Wait for confirmation (30-60 seconds)

### **4. Test Admin Verification (if you want to test full flow)**
1. Go to the **Verify tab** (Admin only)
2. Enter:
   - **NGO ID**: 1 (first registered NGO)
   - **Credits to Allocate**: 1000
3. Click **"Approve"**
4. Confirm transaction

### **5. Test Trading**
1. Go to the **Trade tab**
2. Enter:
   - **Seller Address**: Your wallet address
   - **Credits Amount**: 100
   - **Price (ETH)**: 0.001
3. Click **"Execute Trade"**
